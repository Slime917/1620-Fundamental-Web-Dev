About Café Luna

Founded in 2015, Café Luna has been the cozy neighborhood spot for coffee enthusiasts and pastry lovers alike. Our mission is to create a warm, welcoming environment where everyone can enjoy freshly brewed beverages and baked delights. From our signature lattes to seasonal treats, every item is crafted with care and passion.
